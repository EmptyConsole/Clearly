OPEN QUESTIONS FOR THE OWNER
  1. RELIABILITY TARGET CANNOT BE MET UNDER THE SPECIFIED RULE (M01). The target says at least 80%
     of listeners with an 8 to 12% guess rate should be rated Low. Under the section 4 rule, Low
     needs more than max(2, floor(0.30 n)) false alarms in n catch trials. A 10% guesser expects
     about 0.1 n, so with n = 6 the chance of 3 or more is about 2%, and no number of catch
     trials closes the gap (0.1 n stays below 0.30 n). Track SDs do not help: the estimator stays
     confident. Measured: 1.9% (Express), 0% (Refine). Nothing was changed. Options:
       a. Relax the target (for example "rated Medium or Low", or a lower share).
       b. Change the rule, for example Low when fa >= 2 with n <= 8. This would also rate about 1
          in 50 normal listeners Low and needs re-simulating against the 5% limit.
       c. Add a second signal, such as "heard" answers far below a track's estimate.
       d. More catch trials. This alone cannot reach 80% under the current rule.
     The test is marked it.fails with this explanation until you decide.
  2. EXPRESS UNDER-ESTIMATES BANDS FAR ABOVE THE PRIOR (M01). With 4 trials a track moves only
     about 25 to 30 dB from its prior, and the Gaussian prior makes the result look confident
     (SD about 6 dB). Someone who hears nothing at a band gets a threshold far too low and is not
     flagged. Only 71% of past-ceiling tracks are flagged in Express (86% in Refine). The accuracy
     targets still pass because such listeners are rare in the simulation. Possible fixes, both
     changes to the specified algorithm: a heavier-tailed prior, or an extra trial when every
     answer on a track is the same. Should we change it?
