CLEARLY: STRINGS FOR REGULATORY REVIEW

BEFORE ANY RELEASE: a qualified regulatory professional must review this file for each launch
market (for example the US FDA, the EU under the Medical Device Regulation 2017/745, and the UK
MHRA) before Clearly is released there. Regulatory status is decided by intended use: what the
product, its words, its listings and its marketing say it is for. A disclaimer does not decide it.
Nothing in this file is a regulatory opinion; the developers have not classified the product.

Every user-facing string about results or health, and every claim Clearly makes anywhere (the
app, the Hearing Check PDF, the installer and app metadata, and any store listing), for that
review (RULES.md rule 1). Each entry: message id in apps/web/src/copy/en.ts (or the file), the
text, where it is used, the milestone that added it, review status. Status for every entry: not
yet reviewed.
